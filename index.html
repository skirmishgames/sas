About

A fan-made mission designer for SAS: Rogue Regiment, the solo and co-operative WW2 stealth game designed by Robert Butler and published by Word Forge Games. Build a map from the game's double-sided tiles, place Operators, Axis units, vehicles and objectives, draw patrol and convoy routes, and print a mission sheet laid out like the ones in the mission book.

Mission index

The Missions page lists every mission in the SAS mission book (core, They Who Dare, Heavy Metal, Hot Dogs, Jäger, Kickstarter and Black Box) and SAS Declassified. Search by name, unit or objective, and filter by series, type, event deck, Operators, map size or feature. Tick "Only what I own" to hide missions that need expansions you don't have. Summaries are short and spoiler-light; the full briefing is in your book. "Use as a template" starts a new design with that mission's setup.

Fan Ops

New missions made for the community. Each one is marked Needs playtest until it has 3 playtest reports, and Send feedback opens a ready-made email with the questions for that mission.

Errata and FAQ

The FAQ page carries the official FAQ and errata sheet, searchable and linkable. The two mission errata (Wunderwaffe and Hellraisers) are already applied in the mission index.

How to use the designer

  • Pick a map tile under Pieces and tap a dashed slot. Select a tile, then drag it to move it. Use Flip for the other side and Rotate to turn it.
  • Units stay picked so you can place several in a row. Tap Done when finished. Guards and other Axis units can be rotated to show their facing.
  • Choose a role (Patrol, Convoy, Guard, Assault or Scout) for each Axis vehicle. The border colour and the key label follow the mission book.
  • Draw routes square by square, then tap Finish.
  • Fill in the Mission tab. The mission sheet builds its key from the pieces on the map.
  • Your missions save in this browser. Export one mission or a backup of all of them, and use Import to bring them back or onto another device. Export also makes share links, PNG images and prints.
